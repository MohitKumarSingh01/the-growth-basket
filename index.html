FAQ

Questions brands usually ask.

What does full account management include?

It can include catalog, listings, ads, creatives, A+, Brand Store, inventory, PO/GRN coordination, promotions, account health and performance reporting.

Can you manage multiple platforms together?

Yes. We can coordinate marketplace, quick-commerce and Meta Ads work so the brand has one central growth team.

Do you guarantee sales?

We build strategies around sales growth and performance, but responsible growth depends on product-market fit, pricing, inventory, competition, reviews and platform conditions. We do not present a fixed sales guarantee without an audit.

Is your pricing lower than typical agencies?

We aim to keep our pricing competitive and practical for growing brands. The final scope and fee can be tailored to the number of channels and services required.

Can you start with only ads or listings?

Yes. You can start with a focused service and expand to full account management as your needs grow.
